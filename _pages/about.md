Hi, welcome to my personal page!

I am currently a **Postdoctoral Research Associate** at ***Digital Manufacturing Lab***, The University of Manchester, supervised by [Prof. Charlie C.L. Wang](https://mewangcl.github.io/). 

Prior to this, I received my **Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering** from The University of Manchester in 2024, and my **B.E. in Mechanical Engineering** from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in 2019.

My research interests lie in **computational design, soft robotics, and robot learning**.
